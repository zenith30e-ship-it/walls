WALLS

Reach the opposite side first. On your turn, click a green dot to move one square (you can jump an adjacent pawn), or click a gap between squares to place a wall (2 squares long). You can’t completely block a player’s path. Each player has 10 walls. Squares are named a–i and 1–9; a wall is named by the square it sits above (h) or to the right of (v), e.g. e4h.
